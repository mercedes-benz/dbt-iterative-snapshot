## Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing and merging pull requests
- Triaging issues and community questions
- Ensuring code quality and test coverage
- Managing releases and versioning
- Enforcing the [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- Responding to security vulnerability reports

## Becoming a Maintainer

New maintainers must:

1. Be a Mercedes-Benz employee
2. Have completed all required FOSS trainings (Awareness, Use, Contribute, Create)
3. Be nominated by an existing maintainer
4. Be approved by at least two existing maintainers
